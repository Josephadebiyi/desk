A Sunday with ZionDesk

From first visit
to family — automatically.

Here’s what happens when a guest walks through your doors this Sunday.

1

They scan the QR on the screen

A first-time guest or new convert registers on their phone in under a minute — in their own language.

2

A warm welcome arrives

ZionDesk sends a welcome message by email, SMS or WhatsApp, signed with your church’s name.

3

Your team follows up the same day

New people appear on your dashboard so leaders can call, visit and pray with them.

4

Every Sunday, one-tap check-in

Members check in with the same QR. You see who’s here — and who you’ve been missing.

5

Nobody quietly disappears

When a regular misses Sundays in a row, a caring check-up goes out (if you switch it on). Replies come straight to your inbox.

Giving works the same way. Share your giving QR code — members give by card or mobile money in seconds, and every gift is recorded with a receipt.
